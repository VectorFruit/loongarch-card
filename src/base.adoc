==== LA32R Instruction Formats

.LA32R Base Integer Instruction Formats Table
[cols="1,1,2,2,3", compact, options="header"]
|===
| Inst      | FMT   | Opcode                 | NOTE                            | Description
| ADD.W     | 3R    | `000000 0000 01 00000` |                                 | `GR[rd] = GR[rj] + GR[rk]`
| SUB.W     | 3R    | `000000 0000 01 00010` |                                 | `GR[rd] = GR[rj] - GR[rk]`
| SLT       | 3R    | `000000 0000 01 00100` | signed                          | `GR[rd] = GR[rj] < GR[rk] ? 1 : 0`
| SLTU      | 3R    | `000000 0000 01 00101` | unsigned                        | `GR[rd] = GR[rj] < GR[rk] ? 1 : 0`
| NOR       | 3R    | `000000 0000 01 01000` |                                 | `GR[rd] = ~(GR[rj] \| GR[rk])`
| AND       | 3R    | `000000 0000 01 01001` |                                 | `GR[rd] = GR[rj] & GR[rk]`
| OR        | 3R    | `000000 0000 01 01010` |                                 | `GR[rd] = GR[rj] \| GR[rk]`
| XOR       | 3R    | `000000 0000 01 01011` |                                 | `GR[rd] = GR[rj] ^ GR[rk]`
| SLL.W     | 3R    | `000000 0000 01 01110` |                                 | `GR[rd] = GR[rj] << GR[rk] [4:0]`
| SRL.W     | 3R    | `000000 0000 01 01111` |                                 | `GR[rd] = GR[rj] >> GR[rk] [4:0]`
| SRA.W     | 3R    | `000000 0000 01 10000` |                                 | `GR[rd] = GR[rj] >>> GR[rk] [4:0]`
| MUL.W     | 3R    | `000000 0000 01 11000` | result is low signed 32 bits    | `GR[rd] = GR[rj] * GR[rk]`
| MULH.W    | 3R    | `000000 0000 01 11001` | result is high signed 32 bits   | `GR[rd] = GR[rj] * GR[rk]`
| MULH.WU   | 3R    | `000000 0000 01 11010` | result is high unsigned 32 bits | `GR[rd] = GR[rj] * GR[rk]`
| DIV.W     | 3R    | `000000 0000 10 00000` | signed                          | `GR[rd] = GR[rj] / GR[rk]`
| MOD.W     | 3R    | `000000 0000 10 00001` | signed                          | `GR[rd] = GR[rj] % GR[rk]`
| DIV.WU    | 3R    | `000000 0000 10 00010` | unsigned                        | `GR[rd] = GR[rj] / GR[rk]`
| MOD.WU    | 3R    | `000000 0000 10 00011` | unsigned                        | `GR[rd] = GR[rj] % GR[rk]`
| SLLI.W    | 3R    | `000000 0001 00 00001` |                                 | `GR[rd] = GR[rj] << ui5`
| SRLI.W    | 3R    | `000000 0001 00 01001` |                                 | `GR[rd] = GR[rj] >> ui5`
| SRAI.W    | 3R    | `000000 0001 00 10001` |                                 | `GR[rd] = GR[rj] >>> ui5`
| SLTI      | 2RI14 | `000000 1000`           | `I14[14:13]` as opcode, signed       | `GR[rd] = GR[rj] < SEXT(si12, 32) ? 1 : 0`
| SLTUI     | 2RI14 | `000000 1001`           | `I14[14:13]` as opcode, unsigned     | `GR[rd] = GR[rj] < SEXT(si12, 32) ? 1 : 0`
| ADDI.W    | 2RI14 | `000000 1010`           | `I14[14:13]` as opcode               | `GR[rd] = GR[rj] + SEXT(si12, 32)`
| ANDI      | 2RI14 | `000000 1101`           | `I14[14:13]` as opcode               | `GR[rd] = GR[rj] & ZEXT(ui12, 32)`
| ORI       | 2RI14 | `000000 1110`           | `I14[14:13]` as opcode               | `GR[rd] = GR[rj] \| ZEXT(ui12, 32)`
| XORI      | 2RI14 | `000000 1111`           | `I14[14:13]` as opcode               | `GR[rd] = GR[rj] ^ ZEXT(ui12, 32)`
| LU12I.W   | 1RI21 | `000101`                | `I21[21] = 0`                   | `GR[rd] = si20 << 12`
| PCADDU12I | 1RI21 | `000111`                | `I21[21] = 0`                   | `GR[rd] = PC + SEXT((si20 << 12), 32)`
| LD.B      | 2RI14 | `001010 0000`           | `I14[14:13]` as opcode               | `GR[rd] = SEXT(M[paddr] [7:0], 32)`
| LD.H      | 2RI14 | `001010 0001`           | `I14[14:13]` as opcode               | `GR[rd] = SEXT(M[paddr] [15:0], 32)`
| LD.W      | 2RI14 | `001010 0010`           | `I14[14:13]` as opcode               | `GR[rd] = SEXT(M[paddr] [31:0], 32)`
| ST.B      | 2RI14 | `001010 0100`           | `I14[14:13]` as opcode               | `M[paddr] = GR[rd] [7:0]`
| ST.H      | 2RI14 | `001010 0101`           | `I14[14:13]` as opcode               | `M[paddr] = GR[rd] [15:0]`
| ST.W      | 2RI14 | `001010 0110`           | `I14[14:13]` as opcode               | `M[paddr] = GR[rd] [31:0]`
| LD.BU     | 2RI14 | `001010 1000`           | `I14[14:13]` as opcode               | `GR[rd] = ZEXT(M[paddr] [7:0], 32)`
| LD.HU     | 2RI14 | `001010 1001`           | `I14[14:13]` as opcode               | `GR[rd] = ZEXT(M[paddr] [15:0], 32)`
| JIRL      | 2RI16 | `010011`             |                                 | `GR[rd] = PC + 4, PC = GR[rj] + SEXT(si16 << 2, 32)`
| B         | I26   | `010100`             |                                 | `PC += SEXT(si26 << 2, 32)`
| BL        | I26   | `010101`             |                                 | `GR[1] = PC + 4, PC += SEXT(si26 << 2, 32)`
| BEQ       | 2RI16 | `010110`             |                                 | `if (GR[rd] == GR[rj]) PC += SEXT(si16 << 2, 32)`
| BNE       | 2RI16 | `010111`             |                                 | `if (GR[rd] != GR[rj]) PC += SEXT(si16 << 2, 32)`
| BLT       | 2RI16 | `011000`             | signed                          | `if (GR[rd] < GR[rj]) PC += SEXT(si16 << 2, 32)`
| BGE       | 2RI16 | `011001`             | signed                          | `if (GR[rd] >= GR[rj]) PC += SEXT(si16 << 2, 32)`
| BLTU      | 2RI16 | `011010`             | unsigned                        | `if (GR[rd] < GR[rj]) PC += SEXT(si16 << 2, 32)`
| BGEU      | 2RI16 | `011011`             | unsigned                        | `if (GR[rd] >= GR[rj]) PC += SEXT(si16 << 2, 32)`
| BREAK     | 3R    | `000000 0000 10 10100` | `inst[14:0]=code`               | `raise(BREAK)`
| SYSCALL   | 3R    | `000000 0000 10 10110` | `inst[14:0]=code`               | `raise(SYSCALL)`
|===

NOTE: In LA32R Load-Store Instructions, `vaddr = GR[rj] + SEXT(si12, 32), paddr = AddressTranslation(vaddr)`. The `AddressTranslation()` function is implementation defined.

.LA32R Privilege Instruction Formats Table
[cols="1,1,2,2,3", compact, options="header"]
|===
| Inst      | FMT   | Opcode               | NOTE                            | Description
| CSRRD     | 2RI14 | `00000100`           | `I14 = CSR, rj = 0b00000`       | `GR[rd] = CSR[csr]`
| CSRWR     | 2RI14 | `00000100`           | `I14 = CSR, rj = 0b00001`       | swap `GR[rd]` and `CSR[csr]`
| CSRXCHG   | 2RI14 | `00000100`           | `I14 = CSR, rj != 0, 1`         | swap `GR[rd]` and `CSR[csr]`, `GR[rj]` used as CSR wmask
|===
